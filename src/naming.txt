Maps should end with `Map` and have XxxByYyyMap form.
Sets as Arrays may skip `Set` suffix.


Objects parts should be fully specified:
E.g. when property return array of Booth objects:
GOOD:   boothNames
BAD:    booths


It is OK to skip full names in cases like this:
GOOD:   boothByNameMap
BAD:    boothByBoothNameMap
